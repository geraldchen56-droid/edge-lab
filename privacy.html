Edge Lab

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Edge Lab is a free study site with trading-interview games. You can play every game without an account. This page explains what data the site handles and why.

If you don't sign in

Nothing about you is stored on our servers. Your browser keeps a few settings on your own device (localStorage): which modules you've marked done, sound on or off, your language, which game briefings you've already seen, and guest Daily Gauntlet results. You can clear these at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and is only used to save Daily Gauntlet scores and show them on the leaderboard. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We store:

  • a display name, by default your first name and last initial (for example "Gerald C."), which you can change at any time;
  • your profile picture URL;
  • your Daily Gauntlet results: date, score, number correct, time taken and which questions you got right.

Your display name, picture and scores appear on the public leaderboard. Your email address is never shown publicly. It is kept only by our authentication provider to identify your account.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase stores accounts and scores (server region: Singapore).
  • Google handles the sign-in itself.
  • Vercel hosts the site and provides Web Analytics. Vercel Web Analytics counts page views and visitors in aggregate without cookies and without identifying individual people.

We don't sell your data, show ads or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all your scores, open an issue on the project's GitHub page asking for deletion (you don't need to include your email publicly; we'll arrange it privately). Your data will be removed within 30 days.

Children

Edge Lab is a maths and trading study tool. It contains no real-money gambling. If you are under 13, please don't create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
